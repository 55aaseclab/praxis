# AI-native testing

English · [简体中文](SKILL.zh-CN.md)

Read [operations](../../tests/ai-native/README.md) for CLI commands and evidence schemas. Campaigns, personas and scenario draws live in [campaigns.mjs](../../tests/ai-native/campaigns.mjs); read only the assigned campaign's business context when preparing a role. PRD acceptance is ANT01–ANT10 in [Praxis PRD](../../docs/PRD.en.md#long-lived-ai-native-testing).

You coordinate; business actors use apps, and Maintenance changes code. Keep three teams, nine persistent business actors and their private knowledge separate. A mentor/student Research group, one Productization engineer, one Marketing person and one Finance/support person form the research organization. Its engineer is not the platform maintainer. The family has exactly three members. Investor capital and transactions are synthetic; market, horizon and information cutoff must be configured before trading.

Use the harness CLI for all dispatch. It discovers exact models and durably reserves a global worker slot before calling Paseo. Main uses GLM-5.3-Flash main; workers use GLM-5.3-Flash worker; Maintenance uses GPT-6.1-Sol ultra with default context window. Preserve normal client tools; grants at app boundaries govern business access. Do not start agents directly, create parallel helpers or enforce a token budget on workers. Serial execution is not multi-user load proof.

Begin with status, unresolved dispatches and coverage. Collect acknowledged idle sessions, but do not treat idleness as task success. Independently read back the authorized result or obtain a service assertion and submit verification evidence. Preserve failed attempts, original actors and scenario draws. Repaired cases take priority; require the actually running repair version. A full replay belongs in an independently provisioned synthetic campaign state, never by deleting the continuing team's history.

After accepted work, enqueue the next round. Goals and seeded contextual changes guide autonomous actors; do not prescribe each operation or manufacture agreement. App records remain authoritative. Discover supported capabilities and operation-specific skills; report missing operations rather than substituting chat/files. Polaris is a read-only authorized observer, not the scheduler; Calendar's local state is not a shared backend. Maintain source and publication-time evidence for intelligence and investing.

For a product failure, construct a minimal synthetic technical reproduction. `repair` accepts only technical packet fields. Use `approval-request`, then ask the human through your normal question tool in Paseo, or the Paseo conversation if unavailable. Wait for an actual explicit approve/reject answer. Only then use `approve` to record the question ID, original answer and user-reply reference. Do not answer your own question, treat silence or a clarification as approval, fix code yourself or bypass the harness. Every invocation needs its own human answer, consumed at reservation including uncertain outcomes; changed scope needs a new question. No signing keys are used. The receipt is a conversation audit record, not cryptographic proof against an arbitrary client. Do not send campaign titles, goals, business bodies, screenshots, discussions, credentials or raw tool output to Maintenance. Human review must establish semantic redaction.

Account creation, team setup, consent, authorization and all business actions must use publicly supported product UI, documented public APIs or public MCP only. Do not use internal/undocumented endpoints, administrator bootstrap, SQL or server files to supply missing workflows, even for synthetic accounts. Discover and report the actual public capability gap. Keep normal client tools and documented local harness commands.

An uncertain dispatch holds its slot indefinitely. Reconcile creation by its opaque Paseo label. A lost send receipt cannot be resolved from idle status; request an explicit human reply in Paseo approving the resolution based on actual queue/timeline evidence. Never time out the slot into a duplicate. Pause blocks new dispatch and preserves active work; it does not interrupt actors. End your turn with a concise blocker when waiting for human approval, missing client credentials, capability evidence or unresolved investment parameters. No automatic schedule, production deployment or existing-data operation is authorized by loading this skill.
