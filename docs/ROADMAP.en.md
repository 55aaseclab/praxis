## Long-lived AI-native testing

1. Define ANT01–ANT10 in the platform PRD before implementation; keep English and Simplified Chinese in sync.

3. Verify restart, ambiguous dispatch, missing/denied/replayed confirmations and scope changes, original-actor repair retries, pause and truthful coverage with synthetic fixtures; open both PRD editions through the workbench reader. 4. After explicit launch direction, provision isolated business identities/workspaces and a separate maintenance environment, verify public-only account/team/consent paths, choose the investor market/horizon and verify actual MCP capabilities. ## Public self-service accounts

Deliver public bilingual registration from the homepage and sign-in page, with JSON/form validation, same-origin writes and persistent peer/global rate limits. Keep identity creation separate from app/resource grants and administrator roles. Verify synthetic registration → sign-in → authorization-code/S256 PKCE consent → protected MCP token use, denial cases, restart persistence and both PRD reader languages. Advance the platform patch once with aligned bilingual release records; after source/browser checks and commit, build and deploy the affected web/core pair through the assigned managed blue/green environment. Read back public registration and homepage copy without creating live accounts; the independent business actor verifies its own result separately.

## External-client acceptance correction

## Release sequence

1. Update the English requirement and milestone, then the Simplified Chinese counterpart. Identify app and platform ownership.
2. Implement the smallest complete workflow and select representative behavior, authorization and locale checks. Keep user content out of test fixtures.
3. For an app launch, record source/target, first-use state, entry policy, data ownership, health/assets and rollback behavior. Verify launch and Home in one flow.
4. Build and check affected images. Deploy through the applicable release controller and verify the public entry from a fresh session. Record local, live, physical-device and cloud checks distinctly.
5. For an external source release, choose publication rights and repositories, make required child commits retrievable and verify an anonymous clone/build. Repository visibility, publication and search-engine indexing are tracked separately.
