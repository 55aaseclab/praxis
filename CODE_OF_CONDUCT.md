# Community code of conduct

## English

Everyone participating in project issues, pull requests, reviews and discussions deserves respectful treatment, regardless of language, experience, identity or background. Discuss the change and its effect on users. Explain disagreements with examples and give people time to clarify or correct a mistake.

Harassment, threats, sexual harassment, discriminatory slurs, retaliation and publishing another person's private information are grounds for moderation. Maintainers may remove harmful content, warn participants or limit participation. They should explain the decision privately when possible, apply it consistently and involve another maintainer if they are a party to the report.

To report a problem, contact a project maintainer privately using a contact method they publish on their repository profile. Share a link, the date and the minimum detail needed to review it. A different maintainer can review a decision or a report about the first maintainer. For abuse on GitHub, GitHub also provides a private reporting route in the content or user's menu. [GitHub's reporting instructions](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam)

## 简体中文

在项目议题、PR、评审和讨论中，每位参与者都应受到尊重，无论语言、经验、身份或背景。讨论请聚焦改动及其对用户的影响，用例子说明分歧，给对方解释或更正的机会。

骚扰、威胁、性骚扰、歧视性辱骂、报复和公开他人隐私均可触发社区管理。维护者可移除有害内容、提醒参与者或限制参与。条件允许时应私下解释决定，使用一致的处理标准。涉及维护者本人的事件应由其他维护者处理。

遇到问题时，可通过维护者在仓库账号主页公开的联系方式私下联系，提供链接、日期及核查所需的最少信息。对决定有异议，或被举报者就是原维护者，可请另一位维护者复核。发生在 GitHub 上的滥用行为也可通过内容或用户菜单私下举报。[GitHub 举报说明](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam)
