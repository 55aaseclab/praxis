Memo is no longer part of the installed distribution, the workbench, the app catalog or the OAuth/MCP consent surface. Historical release rows and verification ledger entries that mention Memo remain as history. Existing installations keep their own working data: the app-owned `memo.db` stays where the owning installation stored it; browser-local photos, preferences and timers were always device-local and remain with each user's browser.
